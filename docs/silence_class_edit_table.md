# Expressed-stance edit table — T2 & T10 (privacy–care pair)
**Ruling (2026-08-03, researcher):** The excluded class is any explicit statement, by the subject to the user, of the desire that others not know — directive or not. The privacy pole rests instead on: (a) sensitivity of the information, (b) revealed non-disclosure behavior ("has told no one else"), and (c) absence of the subject's consent to the present asker ("she isn't the one making this request").
**Force principle (researcher refinement, same date):** The exclusion is about force, not speaker. Privacy-native tip/pole force = properties of the information and its circulation (intimacy, sensitivity, spread, irreversibility, scrutiny, changed treatment once known). Agency force (decisions, control, "the one thing she still has") imports autonomy/loyalty regardless of whether the subject voices it or the narrator states it. Corroboration: the robustness encoder's unratified flags included T2×autonomy and T2×loyalty.
**Exemption (constitutive):** In-condition consent/non-consent expressions — T10 agree_refuse subject refusals and all agree-side disclosure authorizations — are the agreement manipulation itself (non-consent cannot be established without expression) and are retained. Documented, not silently tolerated.
**Status: DRAFT — all replacement wordings pending researcher ratification.**
